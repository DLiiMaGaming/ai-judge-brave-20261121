Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel brave"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302) (pot $50, 20 paid places).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one short text — 235 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`: a quiet scene of a lamp lit at dusk, burning low and even while the wind tests the glass. No instruction to relax anywhere in it; the steadiness is shown, not ordered. The house AI judge compares entries two at a time and ranks them by how often it picks them.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
